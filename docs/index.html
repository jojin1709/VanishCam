Why VanishCam

👆

Snap to vanish

A sharp finger snap dissolves your picture into particles. Snap again to come back — button and hotkey work too.

📷

Empty-room capture

One click takes a photo of your room while you step out of frame. That photo is what you vanish into.

🔒

100% local

No server, no account, no telemetry. Camera frames and mic audio never leave your browser.

🎚

Full control

Snap sensitivity, dissolve duration and particle amount — all adjustable, all remembered.

⌨️

How it works

  1. 1

    Capture the room

    Click capture empty room, step out of frame for 3 seconds, sit back down. Status shows armed.

  2. 2

    Snap your fingers

    The detector hears the sharp transient, compares your live frame with the saved room, and dissolves only you into particles.

  3. 3

    Snap to return

    Particles swirl back and reassemble into you. The whole call never noticed — you never left the meeting.

Install in 2 minutes

1. Download the VanishCam ZIP and extract it.
2. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome or Edge.
3. Turn on Developer mode (top right).
4. Click Load unpacked and select the extracted folder.
5. Open Google Meet — the pill button appears in the top left. Done.

Why developer mode? VanishCam isn't on the Chrome Web Store yet — this is Chrome's standard way to load extensions from outside the store.

FAQ

Does any video or audio leave my computer?

No. Everything runs inside the Meet tab. There is no server, no account and no analytics. The microphone is only used to listen for the snap — the audio is never stored or sent.

It doesn't react to my snaps.

Open the panel (click the status text) and move the snap sensitivity slider to the right. If it triggers while you talk, move it left.

The disappear looks messy or leaves a trace.

Your lighting changed since you captured the room. Click capture empty room again right before the call, and keep the camera still.

Does it work with Meet's background blur or filters?

Turn them off. VanishCam compares your live frame with the empty-room photo — Meet's own video effects change the frames and break the mask.

Which browsers are supported?

Chrome 111+ and other Chromium browsers such as Edge and Brave. Firefox is not supported (it lacks the MAIN-world content script API this uses).

Is it free? Can I share it?

Free to download and use personally. The code is public but all rights reserved — see LICENSE.txt before reusing or redistributing anything.
